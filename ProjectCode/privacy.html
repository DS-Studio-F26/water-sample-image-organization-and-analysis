Privacy policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

The Water Sample Image Catalog is a student project of the Clark University Data Science Studio. Anyone can browse, view and download the images without an account. An account is only needed to label images or to use the project's admin tools.

What we collect

Only if you create an account or sign in:

  • your email address;
  • if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture as provided by Google;
  • your role on this site (admin, labeler or viewer), and when your account was created and last signed in;
  • if you use email and password, your password, which is stored only in hashed form;
  • if you are a labeler, the labels you apply: each one is stored with your account ID and the time you applied it, so the team can see how the labeling is going.

We don't collect anything about visitors who don't sign in.

On your device

Your choice of light or dark theme and a few display settings (for example thumbnail size) are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device. The site's fonts are served from the site itself, not from a third-party font service.

Why we collect it

To let you sign in, to decide what each account is allowed to do on the site, and to keep a record of who labeled which images.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored by Supabase, which provides this site's database and sign-in service. Signing in keeps a session token in your browser's local storage until you sign out. The site and images are served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Supabase and Cloudflare may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure their services.

Sharing

We don't sell your information or share it with anyone. Your account details are visible only to the project's admins, and only to manage accounts and to see how many images each labeler has labeled. Exported label files contain the images and their labels, not who applied them.

Deleting your account

Email us and we'll delete your account and its data.
