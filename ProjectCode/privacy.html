Last updated: September 30, 2026

The Water Sample Image Catalog is a student project of the Clark University Data Science Studio. Anyone can browse, view and download the images without an account. An account is only needed to sign in to the project's admin tools.

What we collect

Only if you create an account or sign in:

  • your email address;
  • if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture as provided by Google;
  • your role on this site (admin, labeler or viewer), and when your account was created and last signed in;
  • if you use email and password, your password, which is stored only in hashed form.

We don't collect anything about visitors who don't sign in.

Why we collect it

To let you sign in, and to decide what each account is allowed to do on the site.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored by Supabase, which provides this site's database and sign-in service. Signing in keeps a session token in your browser's local storage until you sign out. The site and images are served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Supabase and Cloudflare may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure their services.

Sharing

We don't sell your information or share it with anyone. It is visible only to the project's admins, and only to manage accounts.

Deleting your account

Email us and we'll delete your account and its data.
