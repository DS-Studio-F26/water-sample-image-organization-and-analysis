Syllabus

The schedule for the course is provided below, including required readings and the assignments. The primary reading material is provided on this website, but it links extensively to and draws material from a wide array of external sources, particularly from the following:

  • Dive into Deep Learning

  • Khallaghi, Eastman, and Estes (2023), A review of technical factors to consider when designing neural networks for semantic segmentation of Earth Observation imagery, arXiv:2308.09221

    This paper is in revision for Transactions in GIS, for which it has been divided into 7 separate papers. We will be reading from the first 3 parts for this class:

    1. Part 1: Basic components of convolutional neural networks and backbone networks
    2. Part 2: Convolutional Neural Networks for semantic segmentation and attention mechanisms
    3. Part 3: Models designed to work with time series and sequences
